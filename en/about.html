About Squadoro

Squadoro is a free, independent app, made by players for FC 27 Ultimate Team players.

What the app does

  • SBCs: it spreads the cards in your club across the current Squad Building Challenges to complete as many as possible, using duplicates and untradeable cards first, and ranks SBCs from best to worst value.
  • Best team: it looks for the starting eleven with the highest rating without sacrificing chemistry.
  • My club: import with screenshots, a CSV file or by hand, plus advice on which cards to keep, submit or sell.
  • Catalogue: the list of current SBCs, updated every evening with their requirements, formation and end date. On the website, you can get a notification when new SBCs arrive.

Your data

Squadoro has no accounts and never connects to your game account. Your club, your SBCs and your settings stay on your device. The details are on the Privacy page.

Free, funded by ads and donations

The app is free. The guide pages may show ads, and you can support the project with an optional donation on Ko-fi. The app itself, its calculation screens and the iPhone app show no ads.

Contact

A mistake in an SBC, an idea or a problem? Write to us right from the app with the “An idea or a bug?” button (at the bottom of the screen or in Settings). You can also open an issue on Squadoro's GitHub page. We read every message.

Getting started

Read Getting started with SBCs, then open the app and import your club.
