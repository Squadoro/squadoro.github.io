Re-roll and Upgrade SBCs: the repeatable SBCs

How to make the most of the SBCs you can repeat without limit, and when they're really worth it.

Updated 11 November 2026 · 2 min read

Some SBCs are permanent and repeatable: Re-rolls, Bronze, Silver or Gold Upgrades… They are there to keep recycling the cards that clutter your club. Used well, they turn dozens of useless cards into a few stronger players.

The staircase idea

Upgrades work in steps: Bronze cards give Silver players, Silver cards give Gold players, and so on. The players you receive, untradeable, go on to the next step or into other SBCs.

When to do them

  • When you have lots of untradeable duplicates of the right quality: it's almost always a win.
  • When you need fodder of a specific rating for another SBC.
  • Not with tradeable cards that sell for more than the SBC gives back.

How many times in a row?

The right question isn't “can I do it?” but “how many times can I do it using only cards I have no use for?”. Beyond that, each extra run costs you useful cards.

Tip After each run, add the players you received to your club: they may be useful for the next run or for another SBC.

Example: clearing a pile of duplicates

You've built up about twenty untradeable Gold duplicates rated between 75 and 82. A Gold Upgrade asks for eleven Gold players: you can do it once with those duplicates, receive higher-rated Gold players, then put the ones you don't need into a score-based SBC. In the end, twenty cards that were clogging your duplicates pile have become a few useful ones.

The maths changes if you have to fill in with tradeable cards: each tradeable card you add has a sale price, and the average reward of an Upgrade rarely covers it.

Frequently asked questions

Are Re-rolls pure luck? The reward is random, but over several runs, the average is what counts. A Re-roll is worth it when what you give is worth less than that average.

How many duplicates should I keep in reserve? Enough to do the SBCs that come out the same evening. The rest can go round in the repeatables.
