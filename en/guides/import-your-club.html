Import your club into Squadoro in a few minutes

Screenshots, a CSV file or adding by hand: the ways to add your cards, and how to keep your club up to date.

Updated 11 November 2026 · 2 min read

To work out your SBCs, Squadoro needs to know your cards. There's no connection to your game account: you add your cards yourself, once, then update what changes.

With screenshots

This is the quickest way. Take screenshots of your club in the game or in the companion app, then drop them into “My club” → “Import cards”. Squadoro reads each card (name, rating, position, version) and matches it with the game's player database. When several players match, the app asks you which one it is.

With a CSV file

If you already have your club in a spreadsheet, export it as CSV with one row per card. The app provides a template to download with the expected columns.

By hand

For a few cards, simply search for the player by name in “Add a card by searching for it” and pick the right version.

Keeping your club up to date

  • After opening packs, use “Quick add after packs”: identical cards become duplicates.
  • After an SBC, “Done” removes the cards you used and offers to add the players you received.
  • Mark your active squad and protect the cards you want to keep: they never go into an SBC.

Tip Your cards stay on your device. Only the screenshots you choose to have read are sent to an AI service, to be read.

Example: a 400-card club

With screenshots that show about twenty cards each, around twenty screenshots are enough. Take them while filtering by quality (Gold, Silver, Bronze) so you don't miss anything, add them in small batches, and check the cards the app flags as uncertain. The whole thing takes about ten minutes the first time.

Frequently asked questions

Does Squadoro connect to my game account? No. The app never asks for your login details and doesn't connect to any account.

Is my data synced between my phone and my computer? It stays on the device, but you can move it with a one-time code or a backup.

What if a card is read wrongly? Tap it in your club to fix its card sheet: rating, position, version or price.
