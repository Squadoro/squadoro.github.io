Chemistry in classic SBCs

How chemistry points are counted, why positions matter, and how to reach the required chemistry with only a few players.

Updated 11 November 2026 · 2 min read

Many classic SBCs ask for a minimum total chemistry, for example 26 points. Each player in position can bring 0 to 3 points, depending on how many teammates share his club, nation or league.

The links that earn points

In recent editions of the game, and in Squadoro's calculations, a player earns points when several players in the team share:

  • his club: 1 point from 2 players, 2 points at 4, 3 points at 7;
  • his nation: 1 point at 2 players, 2 points at 5, 3 points at 8;
  • his league: 1 point at 3 players, 2 points at 5, 3 points at 8.

Points add up without going above 3 per player. ICONS and Heroes usually have 3 points by default and help the other players too. If a rule changes in FC 27, what the game shows is what counts: always check the chemistry in the SBC before submitting.

Position, a requirement you can't forget

A player placed out of position brings no chemistry at all. It's the most common mistake when copying a solution: a defensive midfielder put at centre-back can cost you 3 points in one go.

Reaching the chemistry with few players

In a 7-player SBC, the maximum chemistry is 21 points. You therefore need compact groups: three or four players from the same league and the same nation are better than seven scattered players.

Tip Start by choosing a league and a nation you have lots of players from, then fill in with players from the same league. League points are often the easiest to get.

Example: reaching 26 chemistry with 11 players

Eleven players on 3 points make 33. For 26, most players only need 2 or 3 points. A core of eight players from the same league already gives each of them 3 league points. The remaining three can come from another league if they share a nation with at least one teammate, or if two of them come from the same club.

On the other hand, eleven players from eleven different leagues and nations would have almost no points, even with high ratings. Chemistry rewards groups, not individuals.

Common mistakes

  • Putting a player in the wrong position when copying a solution: he loses all his points.
  • Counting an ICON or a Hero as a player from a specific league when their links work differently.
  • Forgetting that a requirement like “players from the same league: max 3” limits exactly those groups.

Frequently asked questions

Does the manager count in SBCs? SBC squads have no manager slot: only the players' chemistry counts, and that's what Squadoro calculates.

Can a player have more than 3 points? No, a player's chemistry is capped at 3.
