What is Squadoro?

Squadoro is a free app that starts from the cards in your FC 27 club to complete as many SBCs as possible while giving away as little value as possible, and to find your best team. Your data stays on your device. Learn more or open the app.

Squadoro is an independent tool made by fans. It is not affiliated with Electronic Arts or EA SPORTS FC, and is not endorsed or supported by them. Player, club and competition names belong to their owners.
