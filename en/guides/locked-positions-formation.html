Formation and locked positions: making the squad fit as it is

Why a 7-player SBC requires specific positions, and how to check the formation before copying a solution into the game.

Updated 11 November 2026 · 2 min read

More and more classic SBCs don't ask for eleven players but for six, seven or eight. The other positions on the pitch are locked: you can't put anything there. A solution built for eleven players, or for another formation, won't fit as it is.

Why the formation matters so much

Chemistry depends on positions: a winger played as a striker loses his points. If the solution you're following was built in a 4-4-2 while the SBC requires a 3-4-3, some of the players will no longer be in position and the required chemistry won't be reached.

Good habits

  1. Open the SBC in the game and note the formation shown on the pitch.
  2. Count the open positions (the ones with a “+”): that's the number of players to place.
  3. Note which positions are open: for example the three forwards, two midfielders, one centre-back and the goalkeeper.
  4. Check that your solution uses exactly those positions before buying or moving cards.

Tip A screenshot of the SBC in the game is enough to get everything back: formation, number of players and open positions.

Example: a 7-player SBC in a 3-4-3

The SBC pitch shows a 3-4-3, but only seven positions have a “+”: the three forwards, the two central midfielders, the middle centre-back and the goalkeeper. The two wide midfielders and two centre-backs are locked. The maximum chemistry is therefore 21 points, and the team rating is calculated on the players you place.

A solution found online for “eleven players in a 4-4-2” doesn't help you: you need seven players in the right positions, whose chemistry works only between them. It's often easier with a small core of players from the same league up front and in midfield.

Frequently asked questions

Does a locked position count towards the team rating? No, it's empty. You therefore need slightly higher-rated players to reach the same rating as with eleven players.

Can I change the SBC formation? In most recent SBCs, the formation is fixed. If the game lets you choose, pick the one that best matches your players' positions.
