Getting started with SBCs in FC 27

What an SBC is, the difference between score-based and classic SBCs, and a simple way to complete lots of them without spending coins.

Updated 11 November 2026 · 3 min read

Squad Building Challenges (SBCs) are the most reliable way to turn the cards you never use into packs, players or upgrades. The idea never changes: you hand in a group of cards that meets a set of requirements, you submit it, and those cards leave your club in exchange for a reward.

Two main kinds of SBC

Score-based SBCs. Each card is worth a number of points, its item score, which depends mostly on its rating. The SBC asks you to reach a total, sometimes with a minimum or maximum rating per card. Chemistry and positions don't matter here: only the sum counts.

Classic SBCs. You place players on a pitch, in a set formation, while meeting requirements: minimum team rating, total chemistry, a number of players from a league or nation, a number of rare players… Some only need 6 to 10 players: the other positions are locked.

Mistakes that cost you

  • Doing SBCs in the wrong order: one that ends tonight comes before a permanent one, or you may miss its reward.
  • Using a tradeable card that sells well when an untradeable duplicate would have done the job.
  • Going far over the score or rating you were asked for: the extra points are lost.
  • Forgetting that a card can only be used once: if two SBCs need the same player, one of them won't be possible.

A simple routine, every day

  1. Look at the current SBCs and keep the ones whose reward you actually want.
  2. Check which ones you can complete with what you already own, without buying anything.
  3. Do the ones that end soonest first, then the best value ones.
  4. After each SBC, add the players you received to your club: they may be useful for the next one.

Tip New SBCs usually go live every day at 6pm UK time (7pm in most of Europe). One-day SBCs disappear at the same time.

Example: an evening of SBCs

Say three SBCs are live: a score-based SBC that ends tonight and gives a pack of Gold players, a three-challenge league set that runs for two weeks, and a permanent Re-roll. You have about thirty untradeable duplicates, ten or so mid-rated Gold players and a few tradeable cards that sell well.

The right order: first the SBC that ends tonight, using your duplicates of the required rating. Then the first challenge of the set, checking that the other two are still possible with what you have left. Finally the Re-roll, with the duplicates that weren't used anywhere else. The expensive tradeable cards stay aside: they are worth more on the market.

What matters here is not finishing one SBC as fast as possible, but spreading your cards so each one goes where it is worth the most. That is exactly the calculation that becomes painful by hand once you have ten SBCs and several hundred cards.

Frequently asked questions

Can I undo an SBC after submitting it? No. The cards you hand in are gone for good: always check the requirements and the cards before submitting.

Should I do every SBC? No. Many are only worth it if you already have the right fodder. Better to do fewer, well chosen ones.

Are the players I receive useful? Yes: SBC rewards are untradeable, but they can join your team or serve as fodder for other SBCs.
